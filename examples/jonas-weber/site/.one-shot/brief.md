## 5. The look

- **Mood:** Bold.
- **Colour strategy:** black and white (from Mono, the black-and-white camera) with one loud colour used as whole
  fields: the offline band and the big 2 are drenched in it, everything else is ink on near-white. Light by
  default; dark is its own palette through the switch. Brand #ff9500: the orange of a phone's airplane-mode
  switch, and Jonas spent five years making music play in airplane mode. Not blue: blue is the
  default for tech.
- **Type:** Archivo (variable, with the width axis) for display: a grotesque that goes heavy and wide, so the
  three app names can be set like the lettering on a pool's starting blocks. IBM Plex Sans for text: an
  engineer's sans, plain and exact, for a developer who builds instruments (a lap counter, a camera, a planner).
- **Motion:** Lively, effects addon. The most motion goes to the hero rows: the three names arrive one after
  another and their numbers count up. Drawings draw themselves once (lane lines, signal bars). The email button
  follows the pointer a little. Everything else is still.
- **The one bold move:** the three app names, heavy and wide at poster size, as the first screen. Everything else
  is calm black and white around it, and the orange appears only in whole fields.
- **No photos:** the apps are drawn from what they do (a pool lane, a week of watering, a strip of film), never
  as fake screenshots of his apps.
