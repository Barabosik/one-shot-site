# Brief: Kleiner Kaffee

## 1. Facts only they have
- A coffee bar with eight seats.
- Röstergasse 12, Saarbrücken (Germany).
- Run by Lena Hoffmann since 2019.
- Roasts its own beans, every Tuesday.
- In a 5 kg drum roaster, so each batch is small.
- Four things on the counter: espresso €2.40, flat white €3.60, filter €3.20, cardamom bun €3.00.
- Open Tuesday to Saturday 8:00–17:00, Sunday 10:00–16:00; closed on Mondays.
- Sells its beans to take home, by email: hallo@kleiner-kaffee.example.
- The street it roasts on is called Röstergasse (roaster's lane).

## 2. The one job
A coffee drinker in Saarbrücken (walking past, or searching on a phone) arrives wanting to know whether it's open,
where it is and what a coffee costs, and should leave having set off for Röstergasse 12, the route open; the
second job is the beans: leave having emailed for a bag.

## 5. The look
- **Mood:** Warm.
- **Colour:** committed, light. Cream ground (`--mood warm`), dark brown-black ink, roast brown `#6f3b1e` (a
  roasted bean) on the actions, the links and the drawings, and once as a whole field (the Roasting band). No
  terracotta, no green.
- **Type:** Brygada 1918 (500) for headlines: a book face revived from 1918 print, the letters of a printed menu
  or a roaster's bag label. Karla (400/600) for text, hours and prices, plain zeros. Headline about 4× body,
  leading 1.08, tracking -0.01em. No italic word in the headline, no small label over the headings.
- **Motion:** Gentle. The one moment: the roaster drawing settles in on load (fade and a little scale from 0.97);
  sections fade in as they arrive.
- **Focal point:** the drawn drum roaster in the hero.
- **Pictures, planned:** no photos at all (they said no): `"photos": false`. Drawings of their own things, one
  style (front view, flat tones in two or three shades of roast brown, copper and cream, rounded corners): the
  5 kg drum roaster (hero, half the screen), espresso cup, flat white, filter and cardamom bun at row height
  (menu), a bag of their beans (roasting band), eight stools along the counter (the bar, full width; a closer
  crop of three stools on phones). Atmosphere: the roast-brown field.
- **Live:** open-now big over the week's hours, today marked (Visit), and the open-now line under the headline.
- **Action:** "Get directions" (the address on OpenStreetMap): the header button on desktop, the bottom bar on
  phones with the open-now line, and in Visit. Second: "Email for beans" in the roasting band.

## 6. Genericness test
1. Swap the name? No: the drawn 5 kg roaster, Röstergasse and Tuesday are theirs.
2. Phrase headings? No: Menu, Roasting, The bar, Visit.
3. Category default accent? Roast brown is the subject's own colour, not terracotta; cream is the Warm ground.
4. A section because sites have one? No: each answers one of the visitor's questions above.
5. Something to tell a friend? The roaster drawn on the front page; eight stools you can count.
6. Blur test: split, rows, band, one big drawing, table: five different shapes.
7. Thin? Every price is there with a line, the whole week's hours, the address, the email. The beans' kinds and
   prices are missing (section 7): the band says to ask by email.
8. Warm at a glance? Cream, brown, a book serif, soft corners, drawings: yes.
